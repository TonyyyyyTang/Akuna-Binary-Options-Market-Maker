# Recorded development outputs

These vectors were manually copied from HackerRank during development. Cases 5 through 20 are the sixteen scored trading sessions. The first four tests account for the other four points in the overall score.

The files preserve selected versions and include failed experiments. They are repeated observations of the development cases, not a withheld research test set. The final V7B version had the same recorded PnLs and scores as V4I.

The final competition result was outside the top 30. I do not have an exact final rank or the final hidden per-case score to report here.

Use `python3 scripts/check_results.py` to recompute the totals. Prices, capital and PnL are simulation units. Summing PnL over independent sessions does not represent a single account's investment return.

Arena runs are a separate, post-competition measurement. Their scores have different rules and should not be combined with these development totals.
